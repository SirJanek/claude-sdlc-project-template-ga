# D06 — Mentors are found through their own link, plus a catalogue they opt into
- Date, owner: 2026-10-18, Jan
- Context and the options weighed: (A) only the mentor's share link; (B) a public catalogue; (C) the share link as the base plus a catalogue the mentor joins by choice. No recommendation was given: A fits what mentors said, B fits a stranger with an urgent problem, C costs more to build.
- Decision and why: C, Jan's own choice. It keeps the door two mentors asked for and still gives a developer who knows nobody a way in.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: more build in the first release; a catalogue with few opted-in mentors may not help strangers.
- Status: active
