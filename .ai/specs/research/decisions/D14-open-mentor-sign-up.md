# D14 — Open mentor sign-up; invitations are links to the sign-up page
- Date, owner: 2026-10-18, Jan (spec gate Q6, confirmed in grill-me Q7)
- Context and the options weighed: (A) open sign-up at `/sign-up/mentor`, invitations are plain links to it; (B) invitation-only accounts, as the course's DevMentor backlog chose (operator invites in batches of 20). The mentor list (50 names, 29 with a warm contact) suggested B; the first release has no operator functions beyond measures (E05).
- Decision and why: A. Fewer screens in the first release; the first mentors still arrive through personal invitations, which is the channel the board planned.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: anyone can create a mentor account and appear in the catalogue after opting in (R09); revisit if unknown or low-quality mentors show up, which would call for the invitation flow from the course backlog.
- Status: active
