# D08 — The mentor sets the price; commission 10% via the mentor's link, 20% via the catalogue
- Date, owner: 2026-10-18, Jan
- Context and the options weighed: (A) mentor-set price, 10% commission on bookings through the mentor's link and 20% through the catalogue; (B) mentor-set price, flat 20%; (C) platform price bands. Sources: every benchmark lets the expert set the price; Topmate's 10%/20% split by acquisition channel (benchmark 2026-09-04); the founders' board assumed 15–20%.
- Decision and why: A, accepted from the agent's recommendation; it matches D06 and rewards mentors who bring their own audience.
- Consequences, and what would make us revisit it: two rates to explain; the rates are a chosen policy, not tested with mentors or mentees.
