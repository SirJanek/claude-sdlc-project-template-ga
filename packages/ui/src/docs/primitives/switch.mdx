# Switch

## 1. Purpose

A switch turns one setting on or off and the change applies immediately, without a Save button.

## 2. When to use

- Mentor settings after the profile is published: **Listed in the catalogue** (E02-S04, R09; applies right away — PROPOSAL until the spec decides).
- Any single setting where "on" and "off" are the only states and nothing else on the screen needs saving.

## 3. When not to use

| Situation | Use instead | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Inside a form with a submit button | Checkbox | WAI-ARIA APG Switch vs Checkbox |
| One of several options | Radio group | WAI-ARIA APG |
| A change with money or a person affected (decline, refund) | Button + confirmation dialog | R01.3, L3 |

## 4. Anatomy

| Part | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Track | yes | 32 × 18 px; off `--border-strong`, on `--primary` |
| Thumb | yes | 14 px, `--card`, no shadow (R01.1) |
| Hit area | yes | 32 × 24 px (`::before`) |
| Label | yes | describes the setting, not the state |
| Description | optional | 13 px muted, linked with `aria-describedby` |

## 6. States

<Canvas of={Stories.StateMatrixStory} />

| State | Look | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Off | `--border-strong` track, white thumb (5.53 : 1) | WCAG 1.4.11 |
| On (selected) | `--primary` track, thumb on the right (5.91 : 1) | K2 |
| Hover | off → `--foreground`; on → `--primary-hover` | partia 1 decision 5, K2 |
| Focus | 2 px `--ring` outline, 2 px offset | WCAG 2.4.7, decision 3 |
| Disabled | `--border` track | partia 1 decision 2 |
| Loading | not shown: the setting flips at once; if saving fails, it flips back and an Alert says why (PROPOSAL; batch 3) | R02.2 "visible at once" |
| Pressed, error | not applicable | — |

Thumb movement takes `--motion-fast` (PROPOSAL; position is the second cue besides colour, WCAG 1.4.1) and is instant under reduced motion. Why disabled: a description says it, e.g. "Publish your profile first." (WCAG 3.3.2).

## 7. Behaviour and accessibility

- Tab focuses; Space toggles; clicking the label toggles (WAI-ARIA APG Switch).
- Role `switch`, `aria-checked`; the screen reader reads "Listed in the catalogue, switch, on". The label never says "on/off".
- Hit area 32 × 24 px (WCAG 2.5.8) — tested below.

## 8. Content

- Label = the setting in positive form: **Listed in the catalogue**, not "Hide me from the catalogue" (L3).
- At most ~6 words; details go in the description.

## 9. Responsive behaviour

Label left, switch right in settings rows at every width; long labels wrap, switch stays top-aligned.

## 12. Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Switch for a setting saved at once. | Switch inside a form that also has "Save" — use Checkbox. |
| Label names the setting. | Label "On" / "Off" next to the switch. |
| Track and thumb without shadow (R01.1). | Glowing track or shadowed thumb (A01). |

## 13. Related
